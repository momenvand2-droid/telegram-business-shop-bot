V9.5 Anti-Spam Guard
====================

Added a persistent customer anti-spam layer without changing the sales engine.

Default behavior:
- 15 incoming customer messages within 60 seconds => warning 1.
- The burst counter resets after a warning.
- A second burst => warning 2.
- A third burst => warning 3 and internal bot block.
- Blocked customers receive no further automatic replies until admin unblocks them.

Admin commands:
- /spaminfo CHAT_ID
- /unblock CHAT_ID

Optional environment variables:
- SPAM_ENABLED=1
- SPAM_MESSAGE_LIMIT=15
- SPAM_WINDOW_SECONDS=60
- SPAM_MAX_WARNINGS=3

Important:
This is an internal bot block. Telegram Bot API does not make this equivalent to pressing Block on the customer's Telegram account.
