V9.8 Receipt Lock
- Removed the previous lock based on a customer having received an older card.
- Before a receipt is received, customers always receive the currently configured card details, even after the admin changes the card.
- After a receipt photo is received, that customer's payment details are permanently locked and no card number is sent again.
- Receipt lock persists in SQLite because it is derived from stored order status.
- Existing V9.6 voice guard and V9.5 anti-spam behavior preserved.
