V9.3 Card Control
- Added admin-only /changecard CARD | HOLDER command.
- Existing /setcard and /setholder remain available.
- Added admin-only /deletecards command.
- Card-bearing business messages are tracked by business_connection_id, chat_id and message_id.
- /deletecards uses Telegram Bot API deleteBusinessMessages in batches up to 100 messages per chat.
- Deletion status is persisted to avoid repeatedly deleting already removed messages.
- Requires Telegram Business bot right can_delete_sent_messages (or can_delete_all_messages).
